MODE=plain
IR_CONTRACT=vgcf-ir-v2
Convert every numbered theory sentence and q1 into one JSON object using the
VGCF IR v2 contract. The required top-level fields are facts, rules, and query.
Facts and rules retain their s IDs; the query retains q1. Atom values use a
leading + or - and predicate(arguments). Predicates and constants are lowercase.
For a multiword constant, preserve every word and article and join them with
underscores: Bob -> bob; the bald eagle -> the_bald_eagle. Uppercase identifiers
are variables and may occur only inside rules. Return the target JSON object.
